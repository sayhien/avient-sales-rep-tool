# Hue, a sales assistant concept for Avient Specialty Inks

A design challenge by Loo Say Hien. The brief: design a tool for business to business sales reps to check sales progress, prioritise high value deals, spot stalled opportunities and take fast action, without tedious manual documentation.

This is a concept prototype with sample data. It is not an official Avient product.

## The idea in one paragraph

Daniel sells textile screen printing inks in Singapore. He must record what customers say, quickly and correctly. Then he must act on many small things at the right time: promises, deals, payments and his sales target. He records a note in seconds: he talks, types, writes with a pen, or takes a photo of his notebook. Hue reads the note and fills in the customer, the next step and the date. He checks the fields and saves. Each morning Hue shows his visits, his follow ups with the most important first, the customers that do not reply, and how far he is from his target.
