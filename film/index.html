One work day of Daniel, with Hue

The words of the film

Daniel sells printing inks in Singapore.

This is Hue, the assistant on his phone.

Home opens with the weather for his visits.

Storms at three, when he visits Tuas Textile Print. Leave fifteen minutes early.

Below it, his sales for the year, and how much is left.

Today shows the work of the day.

His visits come first, in order of time.

Then his tasks. The most important task is first.

Each task gives the reason. A price asked two days ago, on a deal worth eighty six thousand.

After a visit, Daniel adds a note in seconds.

He can talk. He can type. He can write with a pen. Or he can take a photo of his notebook.

Hue reads the note. It finds the customer, the next step, and the date.

Then it fills in the fields for him.

Daniel checks them, and corrects anything that is wrong.

Nothing is saved until he taps Save.

Hue also watches the deals.

This trial is quiet for sixteen days. A trial usually gives a result within fourteen.

Hue gives the reason, and writes the message. Daniel changes it, and sends it.

Each note goes into the customer record. Deals, promises, people, and the full history.

Phone, tablet, and computer. One light customer relationship management tool.

Hue keeps track. Daniel sells.

The voice says "tasks" and "quiet". In the tool, these are now "Follow ups" and "No reply".
